## Current direction — revision 3

### Knee instrument correction — revision 4

The previous knee illustration showed a fluted spherical burr and a bulbous scope tip. Burrs exist in arthroscopy, but this cartilage-edge cleanup illustration now uses a tubular windowed shaver and a slim rigid arthroscope. This is a generic manufacturer-informed depiction, not a claim that the hospital uses these exact products.

- [Stryker Integrated Arthroscopy Platform](https://www.stryker.com/content/dam/stryker/sports-medicine/products/crossbladeseriescutters/resources/Integrated%20Arthroscopy%20Platform.pdf), printed pages 32–33 (PDF page index 16): the Resector example provides the smooth outer tube, rounded end and elongated side-window geometry; the catalogue lists knee cartilage among its uses. The actual catalogue page was downloaded, rendered and visually inspected.
- [Arthrex 4 mm, 30° rigid arthroscope](https://www.arthrex.com/en/products/AR-3350-4031): the actual product photograph was inspected for its uniform narrow shaft and small flush distal optical face.
- [Arthrex Torpedo shaver brochure](https://www.arthrex.com/resources/LB1-000391-en-US/torpedo-shaver-blades?referringteam=imaging_and_resection), printed knee application page: documents a windowed shaver configuration for chondroplasty.

The instrument references inform only geometry and function. No manufacturer marks or brochure content are included in the generated website asset. The anatomical model, cartilage target and separate CSS overlay remain unchanged.

The spine and knee procedure cards now show exposed anatomical teaching models with adjacent instruments, replacing the revision-2 patient scenes. They illustrate the treatment target, not external skin portals or a complete surgical approach. The joint-center model from revision 2 stays in use.

- [AAOS — Herniated Disk](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/globalassets/pdfs/herniated-disk.pdf): distinguish the protruding disc material from the nearby nerve. Emphasis light belongs on the disc; the nerve must not appear to grow out of or merge into it.
- [AAOS — Articular Cartilage Restoration](https://www.orthoinfo.org/treatment/articular-cartilage-restoration/): depict a localized articular-cartilage defect on a femoral condyle, with tools beside that surface rather than aimed at a ligament.
- [AAOS — Knee Arthroscopy](https://www.orthoinfo.org/treatment/knee-arthroscopy/): use a slender scope and a small working instrument beside the cartilage defect.

## Lumbar biportal endoscopy

- [Choi et al. — How I do it? Biportal endoscopic spinal surgery for treatment of lumbar spinal stenosis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4752582/)
- [Biportal Endoscopic Spinal Surgery for Lumbar Spinal Stenosis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6454273/)

Depict a prone patient and posterior lumbar access. For the selected interlaminar approach, two portals lie cranial and caudal to the target level near the spinal midline. They are on the same operative side, with independent viewing and working instruments directed toward the same target. Avoid a left/right pair straddling the spine, abdominal access, or large laparoscopic trocar towers. Actual placement varies by level, anatomy and approach.

## Knee arthroscopy

- [Ward and Lubowitz — Basic Knee Arthroscopy Part 2: Surface Anatomy and Portal Placement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4040009/)
- [AAOS OrthoInfo — Knee Arthroscopy](https://www.orthoinfo.org/treatment/knee-arthroscopy/)

Depict a supine patient with the knee flexed, the anterior patella and proximal shin recognizable. For the selected standard anterior approach, viewing and working portals flank the patellar tendon below the patella near the joint line. Do not show instruments penetrating the kneecap dome, thigh or posterior knee. The camera and working instrument use separate portals.

## Visual treatment

All four source images use natural colors and neutral lighting. The blue treatment is a separate CSS gradient on the website, so it can be adjusted without regenerating the images. Revision 3 uses the ivory bone models for all cards, including the two procedure cards.
